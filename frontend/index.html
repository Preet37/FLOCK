01 · What is it?

Dozens of stores.
Four platforms.
Messy reports.

The Franchise Reality

A Noah's Bagels franchise group with dozens of stores gets paid by DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, and its own app.

The Data Mess

Every single app sends money and reports in a completely different, messy format under different deals, terms, and rules.

02 · Why does it exist?

Apps don't talk to each other or your register.

Each app is a separate company with its own contract, commission tiers, promos, and refund rules.

DoorDash

Separate commission rates, marketing promo rules, and delivery fee withholdings.

Uber Eats

Different refund clawbacks, merchant fees, and custom payout scheduling.

Grubhub

Unique invoice structures, store placement fees, and regional order terms.

Noah's Register

None of the delivery systems automatically talk to Noah's Bagels register or bank.

03 · When & Where

Lump sums land weekly.
Spread across spreadsheets.

When does it come into play? Every week, when the payouts land in Noah's Bagels bank as lump sums that never match what the register says the stores sold.

Where does it live? In Noah's Bagels accounting team, on a staff accountant’s desk, spread across four app portals, the register, the bank account, the contracts, and a pile of spreadsheets.
